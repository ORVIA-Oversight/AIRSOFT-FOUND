ROLE PACKS

Build for the role as well as the look.

Role packs can cover airsoft/milsim team functions such as Team Lead, 2IC, Rifleman, Gunner, Medic/Simulation Medic and Comms. They describe equipment purpose and event responsibilities without presenting private or current real-world operational doctrine as training.
