# AIRSOFT FOUND — MASTER HANDOVER
Date: 2026-09-30
Status: controlled development / live front-end prototype

## Product position
Airsoft Found is now designed as a global airsoft commerce, build, experience, creator and organiser platform. The public site is the commercial front door. The deeper operating layers remain staged integrations until production backends are connected.

## Live product families represented in the website
- Marketplace: New / Used / Swap / Specialist
- FOUND RANGE: venue discovery, range events, partner model and demand capture
- FOUND BUILDS: reference-led loadouts, role packs, genuine/used/alternative/budget routes
- FOUND CREATOR: contributor idea intake, development pipeline and royalty-ready records
- Organiser: mission licensing, campaign packs, embedded player marshals and event economics
- OVERWATCH CAMPAIGN: persistent callsigns, SENSE, transparent scoring, sensors, drones and media
- Business-in-a-Box / Global: country packs, localisation, white label and controlled deployment

## Non-negotiables
- Demo data must remain labelled until verified.
- No paid verification badges.
- Commission must not control ranking.
- Important assurance decisions remain human.
- Public/community loadout identification must preserve uncertainty and disagreement.
- Immersion never overrides marshal/safety authority.
- Local media capture requires consent, retention and publication controls.
- Global availability is capability-gated by country, not assumed.
