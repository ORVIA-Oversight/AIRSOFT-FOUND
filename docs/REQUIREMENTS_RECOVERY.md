# AIRSOFT FOUND — REQUIREMENTS RECOVERY FROM WORKING CHAT
Date: 2026-09-30

This is a structured recovery of requirements and decisions from the Airsoft Found working conversation. It is intended as the authoritative continuation brief, not a verbatim transcript.

## Original marketplace
Search-first global airsoft sourcing/matching network: New, Used, Swap, Specialist. Persistent Wanted searches, member Locker, seller demand matching, trusted suppliers, Workshop, Pass It On, simple Free / Active membership, transparent match explanations and ORVIA assurance.

## FOUND RANGE
Range route added to unified search and navigation. Venue discovery by activity (zeroing, chrono, electronic targets, Test & Tune, private sessions, demo days, tech days, NVG nights, swap meets), partner profiles, demand capture for unserved searches, product-to-range connection and future Airsoft Found flagship range concept.

## Visual direction
Move away from dark tactical/gamer UI. Use a lighter, vibrant, premium consumer style: cream/white, navy structure, teal/orange/purple/green route colours, large photographic sections, rounded cards and explanatory overlays. Temporary triangular Airsoft Found identity is acceptable until final brand mark.

## FOUND BUILDS / living loadout intelligence
Support vague searches such as identifying a chest rig from a UKSF-inspired look. Build reference-led pages by inspiration, era, role and environment. Preserve disputed identification. Offer genuine, used/surplus, close alternative, budget/repro and specialist routes. Use authorised public/community sources and permitted Discord/forum/community signals. Build My Version should reuse Locker items. Role packs: Team Lead, 2IC, Rifleman, Gunner, Medic/Simulation Medic, Comms and related airsoft/milsim functions.

## Affiliate commerce
External product options may include approved genuine retailers and budget alternatives. Affiliate programmes become tracked outbound commercial routes. Affiliate status must be disclosed and commission must not determine relevance ranking.

## Subscription layering
Release capability progressively rather than exposing everything at once: marketplace/range -> builds -> role packs -> team tools -> scenario packs -> training/after-action tools -> Saber Six / campaign systems.

## Saber Six
Flagship narrative universe in development. Canonical campaign story should be compressible into different event counts while retaining core continuity. Potential organiser licensing.

## FOUND CREATOR
Community members submit mission, campaign, faction, mechanic, prop or game ideas. Approved ideas move through review, development, testing, publication and transparent royalty records. Contributor rights/licence terms must be explicit before sale.

## Organiser model
Organiser buys a mission/campaign operating package rather than a PDF alone. Embedded Player Marshals are clearly identifiable, immersed in the event where appropriate and always retain safety/marshal authority. Overwatch is the command layer. Event economics should show how a licence can be recovered through ticket pricing without promising profit.

## OVERWATCH CAMPAIGN / SENSE
Persistent callsigns and unit history across events even when roster members change. Callsign families such as Alpha/Bravo/Charlie/Delta. Transparent campaign scoring based on defined event actions. Role-based information views. Configurable team/command communications. Drones and sensors feed authorised observations/game-state markers, not hidden or automatic identification of people.

## Managed deployment / Campaign Box
Customer pays up front; planning/configuration period follows. Reusable configured hardware can include connectivity, devices, power, sensors and optional media modules. Hardware is tested and dispatched for the first event, returned, checked and reconfigured for reuse. Software-only, managed deployment and fuller field-capability tiers remain possible.

## OVERWATCH MEDIA
Large sites may use static/360 cameras plus approved player/action/NVG-compatible recordings and drone footage. Full-quality recording is local. After equipment return: ingest, verification, sync, index against event state, AI-assisted selects/edit planning, human editorial approval, then reels/highlights/feature film/social/website assets through ORVIA Socials. Media consent, minors, opt-outs, retention and no-camera zones must be designed into booking.

## Business-in-a-Box
White-label operating package for organisers/sites/teams/ranges. Customer supplies brand/country/business configuration; system provisions website, commercial workflow, event operations, Overwatch, Campaign Box and media/social workflows. Follow ORVIA's internal controlled-development conclusion: one narrow validated founding pilot before universal claims.

## Global-first
Airsoft Found is designed for international use from the start. Country packs control language, currency, payment availability, marketplace capability, affiliate/supplier routes, local rules, hardware deployment and support. Digital global reach can expand faster than physical marketplace and managed hardware deployment.

## Outstanding production requirements
Backend/auth/data; actual payments; real affiliate relationships; verified product/venue feeds; legal/terms; creator agreements; royalty ledger; booking; real scoring; hardware standardisation; media pipeline; country capability registry; final 10-image marketing pack; final consumer logo; live commercial purchase/onboarding journey.
