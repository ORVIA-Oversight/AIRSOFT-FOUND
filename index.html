FOUND COMPATIBILITY

What fits what — with the exact variant kept intact.

Airsoft names are messy. Licensed brand, model, generation, calibre, power system and slide or platform variant can all change the answer. Found should show the evidence, exclusions and uncertainty rather than treating similar names as identical.

YOUR EXACT SETUP

Umarex Glock 17 Gen 5 MOS

6 mm airsoft · CO₂ · factory MOS variant. Umarex is the user-facing manufacturer/licensed brand. Any OEM relationship is recorded separately and only when a source supports it.

WHAT YOU WANT TO FIT

Aimpoint ACRO P-2

The search resolves the optic footprint and checks adapter claims against this exact pistol variant rather than mixing real-steel, 4.5 mm and other airsoft platforms.

HOW FOUND ANSWERS

Fits · Does not fit · Needs adapter · Unconfirmed

Every compatibility claim should carry a source, evidence grade and last-checked date. When the exact CO₂ fit is not proven, Found should say so.
